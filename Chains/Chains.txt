Chains in langchain is an important component. 

-------- + ----------------------------------+
Chains makes it easier to make any ai application more convienient to develop

Chains mostly uses Langchain Expression Lnaguage (LCEL) that helps in making pipelines that reduces mannual code and 
minimize the efforts more effectively.

There are 4 main types of Chains in Langchain 

1. Simple Langchain 
  +------+
  |Prompt|  --> {LLM Model} --> Parser(Structured Output)
  +------+

2. Sequential Lnachain Chains 
  +---------+
  |Prompt_1 |  --> {LLM Model} --> Parser(Structured Output) -->|Prompt_2| -->  |LLM Model| --> [ Parser ]
