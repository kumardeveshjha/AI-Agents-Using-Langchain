#### I. Models 
 Langchain has 2 types of model 

 1. Language Models(LLMs)             2 Chat model                            2. Embeddings
(Text in --> Text Out )     i. Closed source and ii. OpenSource       Vectors and Sementic Search 

1. LLM Model are the stateless model we use them directly call the model (Not used that much)
2. Chat Models are the fine tuned models that are built on top of LLMs
   i. Closed Source --> Typically Fined tuned by the Top companies in their premise, mostly they are paid and access   
      through APIs 
   ii. Open Source models are mostly trained, fine tuned and leave to the public for free to use or we can use through   
       huggingFace and openRouter

3. Embedded models are mostly used to make vectors of the given doc or texts.
